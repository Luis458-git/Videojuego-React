## Brand & Style

This design system establishes the visual framework for a cyber-noir mystery investigative interface anchored by an unexpected protagonist: an adorable, squishy dinosaur sleuth operating within a gritty, neon-soaked digital crime agency.

The design philosophy balances **Tactile Neomorphic/Squishy Toy Metaphors** with **Eccentric Cyber-Noir Terminal HUDs**. High-tech digital evidence boards, faint scanline overlays, glowing telemetry feeds, and encrypted holographic data grids collide with pillowy physical touches—chunky case file tabs, plush badge pins, and taped sticky-note evidence markers.

The user interface balances sleek, cold deductive computing with charming, tactile warmth. The emotional tone evokes late-night detective intrigue, playful curiosity, and the satisfaction of piecing clues together on an illuminated glass workstation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around deep night-time investigation tones punctuated by spectral neon glows and playful plush toy accents:

- **Primary (`#A855F7`)**: Signature ultraviolet pompom hue. Used for critical breakthroughs, primary interactive calls-to-action, holographic selection rings, and high-tier mystery reveals.
- **Secondary (`#22D3EE` / `#38BDF8`)**: Cyan investigation laser and HUD telemetry. Illuminates terminal wireframes, active scanline borders, data channels, and deductive link nodes.
- **Tertiary (`#FACC15`)**: Evidence glow and detective amber. Applied to critical case tags, magnifying glass reticles, sticky-note suspect memos, and unread case alerts.
- **Neutral & Surfaces (`#060B16`, `#0B1424`, `#101D30`)**: Obsidian deep noir navy provides pure contrast, tiered into stacked translucent layers that simulate illuminated glass screens hovering in an interrogation darkroom.
- **Signal Tones**:
  - **Success Emerald (`#22C55E`)**: Solved deductive pathways, unlocked dossiers, biometric match confirmations.
  - **Alert Crimson (`#EF4444`)**: Contradiction alarms, locked security clearance, suspect hostility spikes.
  - **Text Primary (`#EAF6FF`) & Muted Slate (`#94A3B8`)**: High-contrast, fatigue-free typographic hierarchy designed for prolonged terminal reading.

## Typography

The typographic pairing communicates the tension between playful character personality and forensic machine readouts:

- **Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`)**: Unapologetically quirky, expressive, and thick. Its exaggerated ink traps and energetic curves echo the stubby, squishy silhouette of the dinosaur detective while commanding strong display presence over dark surfaces.
- **Body & Labels (`Space Mono`)**: A sterile, retro-futuristic monospaced workhorse. Delivers clinical evidence descriptions, timestamped police logs, cryptographic decrypted sequences, and mechanical HUD labels.
- **Typographic Treatment**: Headlines utilize tight letter spacing and high weight (`700`/`800`) to evoke detective pulp mastheads. All machine labels, telemetry readouts, and dossier codes run uppercase in `Space Mono` with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a dual-mode structural grid:
1. **The Investigation Board Canvas**: A flexible, freeform node-canvas supporting zoomable clue mapping, evidence pinning, and holographic yarn strings connecting suspects.
2. **The Terminal UI Shell**: A responsive 12-column grid on desktop (reflowing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile) housing persistent detective toolbars, inventory bags, and transcript feeds.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Base spacing operates on a **4px/8px modular scale**. Dense terminal modules (dossier specs, evidence item inventories) favor `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px) for a compact HUD feel.
- Outer canvases and detective case dossiers breathe with generous macro padding (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), framing focal assets like suspect holographic busts and forensic minigames.
- Mobile layouts employ edge-to-edge docking for tool trays with fixed bottom navigation ribbons (`margin: 1rem`) to keep thumbnail touch targets reachable during handheld sleuthing.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics a multi-layered cyberpunk detective console:

1. **Backdrop Layer (`#060B16`)**: Canvas floor with a subtle CRT horizontal scanline overlay (2px repeating pattern at 4% opacity) and faint cyan grid coordinates.
2. **Elevated Trays (`#0B1424`)**: Translucent panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and 1px borders tinted in `#22D3EE` at 15% opacity.
3. **Floating Dossiers & Interactive Modals (`#101D30`)**: Features dual-light physical displacement:
   - Upper edge specular highlight: 1px inner inset line (`rgba(234, 246, 255, 0.15)`).
   - Peripheral evidence glow: Diffused atmospheric drop shadow colored by context (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)` for primary interactions; `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.20)` for data analysis).
4. **Squishy Tactile Layer (Evidence Chips & Plush Badges)**: Employs a soft dual drop shadow—a deep dark blur underneath (`0 10px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`) paired with a tight neon rim light to simulate a real, squeezable toy accessory resting atop cold surveillance glass.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 3` (pill-shaped foundation), the shape language delivers the tactile, friendly squishiness of the dinosaur detective:

- **Pill Shells**: Buttons, badges, clue tags, and search inputs utilize full pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / 9999px or 1rem to 3rem).
- **Chubby Containers**: Clue cards, diagnostic panels, and dialog bubbles adopt hyper-curved corners (`2rem` / `32px` on desktop; `1.5rem` / `24px` on mobile).
- **Physical Cutouts**: To avoid losing the cybernetic edge, rounded containers feature micro digital glyphs, pill-shaped perforations, and segmented status notches along their outer edges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Squish Button**: Pill-shaped capsule filled with `#A855F7`. Features an inner top highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`) and an active-press spring animation (`transform: scale(0.94)` and vertical depression). Text is uppercase `Space Mono` (`label-lg`) set in `#EAF6FF`.
- **Holo-Cyan Secondary Button**: Outlined in 1.5px `#22D3EE` with a `#0B1424` glass background. Glows cyan on hover with a micro scanline sweep animation across the surface.
- **Plush Badge Pins**: Circular or pill-shaped buttons with embroidered stitch-pattern dashed borders (`stroke-dasharray: 3 3`) that trigger case notes or quick inventory slots.

### Chips & Clue Tags
- Segmented pills housing category icons (e.g., magnifying glass, footprint, encrypted disk).
- Styled in `#101D30` with colored border accents mapping to clue status: Purple (Forensic Clue), Cyan (Suspect Alibi), Yellow (Sticky Lead), Emerald (Verified Fact).
- Micro terminal serial codes rendered in `label-code` format (`#94A3B8`) prepended before each tag title.

### Input Fields & Forensic Prompts
- Terminal capsules with deep background `#0B1424`, bordered by a subtle 1px cyan rim (`#22D3EE` at 25%).
- Blinking block terminal cursor in `#38BDF8`.
- Monospaced placeholder text in `#94A3B8` that renders with a simulated decryption typing animation upon initial focus.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Dino Footprint Checkbox**: Rounded square container (`12px` radius) where checking stamps a playful glowing neon dinosaur footprint in `#A855F7`.
- **Cyber-Capsule Toggles**: Pill track (`#0B1424`) with an illuminated sliding pompom thumb that shifts from cold steel slate (`#94A3B8`) to neon cyan (`#22D3EE`) or ultraviolet (`#A855F7`).

### Evidence Cards & Dossiers
- Heavy rounded containers (`rounded-2xl` / `32px`) resting at `#101D30` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Top header band featuring crime scene index numbers, a sticky-note pin accent in `#FACC15`, and an embedded biometric waveform.
- Hovering over cards invokes an iridescent chromatic aberration glitch effect along the outer glow border.

### Detective Board Yarn Links
- Vector connectors between evidence cards rendered as glowing neon bezier cords (`2px` stroke) pulsing with moving light packets that demonstrate deductive causality.
